About

The name is the method.

CuttingKnots is an IT consulting and AI automation company.

Cut it, don't untie it thread by thread.

Every company has a knot: a process done by hand for years, two systems that don't talk, a report that eats two days a month. People often try to untie it one thread at a time, and months go by.

We prefer to cut it. We look at the problem closely, choose the cleanest possible intervention and take it to production quickly, together with the team who will use it every day.
